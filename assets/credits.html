Graphics & licences

Earth textures by Solar System Scope, based on NASA imagery. CC BY 4.0. Surface, night and cloud maps converted from the original 8192×4096 sources to WebP; mobile surface/cloud maps resized to 2048×1024 and night/elevation maps to 1024×512. Elevation and roughness use the packed map from the official Three.js examples.

Three.js r170 — MIT licence. Full licence.

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are served through Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

The star field and nebula are generated procedurally in the browser. Static error fallbacks are captures of the same scene; normal loading does not show an Earth photograph. Website screenshots show Virentora demonstration projects.
